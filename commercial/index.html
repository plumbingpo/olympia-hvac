Food Service Comfort

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest spaces to keep comfortable. Every burner and fryer adds heat, the exhaust hoods pull conditioned air outside nonstop, and the makeup air system has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. Olympia HVAC sizes, repairs, and maintains the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we schedule service around your hours.

Many restaurant comfort complaints trace back to air balance. When a kitchen runs negative, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, sucks hot or cold outdoor air through every door, and leaves staff miserable during the rush. We balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and document every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. When a job can't happen during service, we do it after close, keeping your kitchen cooking and the dining room comfortable in the meantime.

Inspections & Documentation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm correct venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent service schedule.

Rapid Response

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

An HVAC emergency on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, empty a retail floor on a summer afternoon, overheat a server room, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Olympia HVAC provides fast, emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Olympia and surrounding areas. When you call our commercial line, we dispatch an experienced technician quickly, because every hour without climate control costs you money.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. It might be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, a faulty ignition system or gas valve, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board lockout. Whatever it is, we find it fast, restore heating or cooling, and record every step for your facility files. Afterward, we put maintenance and monitoring in place so the same failure doesn't return.
